Dimen­sions

Fly through geometric worlds shaped by your music. Big drops warp you into the next dimension. Move your phone to look around.

Apple Music: start a song, come back here and tap Listen. Dimensions hears it through the microphone, so play it out loud (it can't hear headphones). Works with any music or sound nearby.

Hibernation room: put the phone down where you rest. It reads the sound and vibration around you, shows what's there, and tells you if your surroundings are good for rest.

Vibration
Symbolic reading of the sound, not a measurement
